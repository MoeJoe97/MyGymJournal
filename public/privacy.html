Data we process

MyGymJournal uses Firebase Authentication for email/password sign-in, email verification and technically necessary authentication persistence. We process your email address, nickname and Firebase user ID. Workout entries may contain exercises, repetitions, workout weight, RPE, estimated 1RM, training volume, timestamps and training dates. We also process body-weight entries and app-generated onboarding state.

Training drafts and preferences (weight unit, chart period and rest duration) may be stored locally on your device.

Services and storage

Firebase/Google provides authentication and Cloud Firestore. Firestore is configured in europe-west3. Hosting provider details: [HOSTING_PROVIDER_DETAILS]. Service providers may process data outside the EU; this draft does not make a legal conclusion about that processing.

There is currently no Google Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Performance Monitoring, advertising, behavioral tracking or BigQuery connection. Necessary infrastructure security and access logs may be created by service providers.

Account deletion limitation

The current deletion flow runs client-side and deletes the authenticated user's Firestore data before deleting the Firebase Authentication account. Before public production release, replace it with an authenticated server-side deletion function for more reliable and centrally controlled deletion.
